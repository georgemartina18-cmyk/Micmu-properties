## 2. Information handled and purposes

Public pages can be viewed without an account. Features you use may involve:

- Enquiries/support: name, email, phone, optional WhatsApp number, contact preference, message, request type and processing status. Inspection requests also include a property, preferred date/time and attendee count. These details support responding and arranging requested services.
- Accounts: email, profile name, account identifiers, authentication/session information, permissions and MFA information where enrolled. Authentication is handled through Supabase.
- Account services: saved properties/searches, notifications and read status, requests, assigned documents and payment records.
- Realtor services: application/contact details, experience, previous-sales count, referrer code, review status, referral relationships, sales, instalments, receipts, agreement notes and commission records.
- Administration: staff permission changes, audit events and information relating to approved public media or company profiles.
- Technical/security processing: providers may handle IP addresses, URLs, device/browser details, timestamps and authentication/security events to deliver and protect the service. Enquiry identifiers and a derived email hash support duplicate and rate-limit checks; the stored enquiry is not anonymous.

These records support requested services, accounts, staff administration, transaction record-keeping and security. Form acknowledgement does not itself authorise marketing. Staff review application and transaction statuses; a calculator result or submitted application does not automatically approve a transaction.

Do not send passwords, MFA codes, card details or identity documents through public enquiry forms. Ask MICMU about an appropriate secure route if documents are needed.

## 3. Access, service providers and external services

Authorised staff can access records within their permissions. Approved realtors can see their direct referrals’ names/statuses and their permitted sales/commission records. Approved public biographies, media, testimonials and verification labels can be visible publicly.

Cloudflare provides website delivery/hosting and security, including Turnstile checks on configured enquiry forms. Supabase provides authentication, database and storage services. Authentication email requests pass through Supabase Auth and its configured delivery arrangements. Existing media may use Cloudflare R2 where configured. Other configured image hosts can receive browser requests when images load.

WhatsApp, Facebook, OpenStreetMap and video/tour links lead to external services when selected. Their privacy practices apply to their processing. Providers may process information outside Nigeria. This interim document does not specify the deployment regions, email delivery provider, additional operational recipients or transfer safeguards.

## 4. Cookies and browser storage

Authentication cookies maintain sign-in and session verification/refresh. Exact names and lifetimes depend on deployed settings. Clearing cookies may require signing in again.

Local browser storage remembers:

- `micmu-lite`: Lite Mode preference.
- `micmu-recently-viewed`: up to 12 property slugs, with a dashboard clear-history control.
- `micmu-referral`: a referral code for a realtor application.

These local values have no application-defined expiry and may remain until replaced or cleared through the browser. Clearing local history does not delete submitted enquiries or account records. Saved searches/properties are stored separately in the account database.

The service worker caches the public offline fallback and an illustration. Its implemented offline cache excludes private dashboards/documents, APIs and live property pages. Ordinary browser/provider caching is separate.

No advertising pixel, Google Analytics or optional analytics beacon was found in the inspected application source. That does not establish whether dashboard-injected analytics or provider security cookies are active. Search Console DNS verification does not add a visitor-tracking script. Blocking security scripts may prevent form verification from working.

## 5. Payments and the calculator

The website has no checkout or payment gateway. Payments are arranged directly with MICMU. Confirmed plans/rates apply to all sale properties. Calculator estimates do not create a purchase contract; the transaction requires MICMU’s written agreement.

Calculator inputs are processed locally in the browser without a calculation-submit API. Ordinary page-request metadata may still reach hosting providers. Staff-entered sales, receipts and payment/commission records are separate database records.

## 6. Retention and safeguards

This interim document does not specify retention periods or a deletion timetable. No scheduled general deletion programme was identified in the inspected code. A short-lived document link does not delete its underlying document; the enquiry rate-limit window is not a retention period.

The implementation includes permissions, staff MFA, database access restrictions and short-lived private document links. These reduce risk without guaranteeing absolute security. There is no general self-service account-erasure function.
