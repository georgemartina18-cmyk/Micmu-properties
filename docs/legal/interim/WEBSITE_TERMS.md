Listings, descriptions, photographs, plans and project updates provide information for enquiries. Saving a property, sending an enquiry or using a calculator does not reserve a property or conclude a purchase. Internal reference checks confirm published MICMU records; they do not replace government title searches or establish a representative’s authority to receive a particular payment. Obtain appropriate independent professional checks before a transaction.

## 3. Requests and inspections

Supply accurate information you are entitled to share. A successful form submission means a request was saved. Inspections remain pending until MICMU confirms arrangements. Contact the team about access, rescheduling, cancellation and any applicable charges before relying on an appointment.

The website does not promise automatic email/WhatsApp notifications or a particular response time for enquiries or status changes.

## 4. Payment plans and written agreements

There is no online checkout or payment gateway. Arrange payments directly with MICMU and confirm authorised payment instructions through its verified contacts. Do not submit card information, bank login details or payment/authentication codes through public forms.

The confirmed plans apply to all sale properties:

- Outright: no surcharge.
- Three months: no surcharge.
- Six months: 5% surcharge.
- Twelve months: 10% surcharge.

The confirmed instalment allocation schedules remain unchanged. The calculator illustrates the amounts using the entered price and those confirmed plans. “Estimates” means calculator use does not create a purchase contract, reserve a property or approve credit; it does not mean the confirmed rates are unapproved.

A final transaction requires MICMU’s written agreement specifying the property price, payment schedule, charges and any expressly agreed discounts. Confirm deposits, refunds, cancellations, defaults and other transaction conditions in the relevant agreement. These website terms introduce no no-refund rule, forfeiture or late fee.

Dashboard payment/receipt records are entered by authorised staff and do not mean the website received or settled a payment. Report discrepancies to MICMU.

## 5. Accounts and submissions

Keep sign-in details secure and contact MICMU about suspected unauthorised use. Never share passwords, reset links or MFA secrets/codes. Staff permissions and MFA protect administrative access.

Do not access another person’s records, bypass security, interfere with the site, submit fraudulent information or upload unlawful material. Share only material you are authorised to provide. Keep private document links private and use an agreed secure route for sensitive documents.

Public team biographies do not grant login access. A general self-service account-deletion function is not currently provided; contact MICMU with account requests.

## 6. Realtor services

An account, referral or application does not itself authorise a person to represent MICMU, receive money or bind the company. Confirm representative authority directly.

A recorded sale or displayed commission does not guarantee payout or replace the relevant written agency/commission agreement. Separate commission arrangements must be confirmed; the sale-property payment plans above are preserved.

Confirm time-sensitive information directly with MICMU.
